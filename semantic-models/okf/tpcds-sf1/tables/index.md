# Tables

* [Call Center](call_center.md) - Versioned operating and geographic attributes for catalog call centers.
* [Catalog Page](catalog_page.md) - Catalog page, department, catalog number, and active-date attributes.
* [Catalog Returns](catalog_returns.md) - Returned catalog line items, refund roles, return reasons, and financial effects.
* [Catalog Sales](catalog_sales.md) - Catalog order line items with billing, shipping, fulfillment, promotion, and financial measures.
* [Customer](customer.md) - Customer identity, profile, birth attributes, contact details, and current dimension pointers.
* [Customer Address](customer_address.md) - Postal, geographic, time-zone, and location-type attributes for customer addresses.
* [Customer Demographics](customer_demographics.md) - Reusable combinations of gender, marital status, education, credit, purchase estimate, and dependent counts.
* [Date](date_dim.md) - Calendar and fiscal attributes used by all sold, shipped, returned, effective, and access date roles.
* [Household Demographics](household_demographics.md) - Household income-band, buying-potential, dependent-count, and vehicle-count combinations.
* [Income Band](income_band.md) - Lower and upper household-income bounds used by household demographics.
* [Inventory](inventory.md) - Daily on-hand inventory snapshots by item and warehouse.
* [Item](item.md) - Versioned product identity, hierarchy, manufacturer, price, physical, and manager attributes.
* [Promotion](promotion.md) - Promotion identity, active dates, cost, target, channels, purpose, and discount state.
* [Return Reason](reason.md) - Standard reason codes and descriptions used by return facts.
* [Ship Mode](ship_mode.md) - Shipping type, code, carrier, and contract attributes for catalog and web fulfillment.
* [Store](store.md) - Versioned store operations, organization, market, geography, address, time-zone, and tax attributes.
* [Store Returns](store_returns.md) - Returned store line items with customer context, reason, refund disposition, and net loss.
* [Store Sales](store_sales.md) - Point-of-sale line items with customer context, promotion, store, and financial measures.
* [Time](time_dim.md) - Time-of-day attributes including hour, minute, second, AM/PM, shift, and meal period.
* [Warehouse](warehouse.md) - Warehouse identity, capacity, address, geography, and time-zone attributes.
* [Web Page](web_page.md) - Versioned web-page identity, lifecycle, ownership, URL, type, and content-volume attributes.
* [Web Returns](web_returns.md) - Returned web line items, refunded and returning customer roles, reason, and financial effects.
* [Web Sales](web_sales.md) - Web order line items with billing, shipping, site/page, fulfillment, promotion, and financial measures.
* [Web Site](web_site.md) - Versioned website operations, market, organization, address, time-zone, and tax attributes.
